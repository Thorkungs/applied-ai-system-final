# Mood guide

Short reference notes on each mood label used in the song catalog. `related`
lists the moods that feel closest, and `typical_energy` is the usual energy
level of songs with that mood on the catalog's 0 to 1 scale.

## happy
related: euphoric, energetic
typical_energy: 0.75
Happy songs are bright, warm and feel good. They are close to euphoric songs, which are happy at full volume, and to energetic songs.

## chill
related: relaxed, focused
typical_energy: 0.35
Chill songs are calm, unhurried and low pressure. They sit next to relaxed songs and to focused songs, which are chill music made for concentrating.

## intense
related: energetic, angry
typical_energy: 0.92
Intense songs are hard hitting and driving, made to push you. They are close to energetic songs and, at the aggressive end, to angry songs.

## relaxed
related: chill, nostalgic
typical_energy: 0.40
Relaxed songs are easy going and comfortable, like an unhurried morning. They are close to chill songs and to gently nostalgic ones.

## moody
related: melancholy, nostalgic
typical_energy: 0.60
Moody songs are dark, atmospheric and thoughtful without being fully sad. They lean toward melancholy and nostalgic songs.

## focused
related: chill, relaxed
typical_energy: 0.40
Focused songs are steady and unobtrusive so they do not pull attention away from work. They are close to chill and relaxed songs.

## confident
related: energetic, intense
typical_energy: 0.80
Confident songs are bold and swaggering. They are close to energetic and intense songs.

## angry
related: intense
typical_energy: 0.95
Angry songs are loud, aggressive and cathartic. Intense songs are their closest neighbor.

## melancholy
related: sad, moody
typical_energy: 0.30
Melancholy songs are quiet, sorrowful and reflective. They are close to sad songs and to moody ones.

## nostalgic
related: melancholy, relaxed
typical_energy: 0.50
Nostalgic songs look back warmly on the past. They are close to gently melancholy songs and to relaxed ones.

## euphoric
related: happy, energetic
typical_energy: 0.92
Euphoric songs are peak joy, hands in the air moments. They are close to happy and energetic songs.

## romantic
related: relaxed
typical_energy: 0.50
Romantic songs are warm, intimate and slow burning. Relaxed songs are their closest neighbor.

## sad
related: melancholy, moody
typical_energy: 0.35
Sad songs are about loss and heartache. They are close to melancholy songs and to moody ones.

## energetic
related: intense, euphoric, happy
typical_energy: 0.88
Energetic songs are lively and fast moving. They are close to intense, euphoric and happy songs.
