# Genre guide

Short reference notes on each genre VibeFinder knows about. `related` lists the
closest neighbors that exist in the song catalog, and `typical_energy` is the
usual energy level on the catalog's 0 to 1 scale. These notes are hand written
for this project, like the song catalog itself.

## pop
related: indie pop, r&b, synthwave
typical_energy: 0.80
Pop is hook driven and polished, built around a big sing-along chorus and bright production. It shares its catchy melodies with indie pop, its smooth vocals with r&b, and its synthesizers with synthwave.

## lofi
related: ambient, jazz
typical_energy: 0.40
Lofi is slow, dusty, low fidelity beat music with soft drums and warm keys, usually without vocals. It is made to sit in the background. It borrows its chords from jazz and its calm, drifting texture from ambient.

## rock
related: metal, blues
typical_energy: 0.85
Rock is driven by electric guitars, bass and live drums with a strong backbeat. It grew out of the blues, and its heaviest and loudest form is metal.

## ambient
related: lofi, classical
typical_energy: 0.25
Ambient music is slow, spacious and mostly beatless, built from long pads and textures. It is close to lofi in calmness and to classical in its patience and lack of vocals.

## jazz
related: blues, lofi, r&b
typical_energy: 0.40
Jazz is built on improvisation, swing and rich chords, played on instruments such as saxophone, piano and upright bass. It shares its roots with the blues, and its harmony shows up again in lofi and r&b.

## synthwave
related: edm, pop
typical_energy: 0.70
Synthwave is retro electronic music inspired by 1980s film scores, with pulsing synthesizers and a neon, night time atmosphere. It is a slower cousin of edm and shares its melodic hooks with pop.

## indie pop
related: pop, folk
typical_energy: 0.70
Indie pop keeps the catchy choruses of pop but with jangly guitars and a more home made, less polished sound. Its quieter side leans toward folk.

## hip hop
related: r&b, lofi
typical_energy: 0.75
Hip hop is rhythm first music built on rapped vocals, sampled loops and heavy drums and bass. It overlaps with r&b in its grooves and gave lofi its drum loops.

## metal
related: rock
typical_energy: 0.95
Metal is the heaviest, loudest branch of rock, with distorted downtuned guitars, double kick drums and aggressive vocals.

## classical
related: ambient
typical_energy: 0.25
Classical music is composed for orchestral instruments or solo piano, usually without drums or vocals, and often slow and dynamic. Its quiet, patient pieces sit close to ambient.

## country
related: folk, blues
typical_energy: 0.55
Country is storytelling music with acoustic and steel guitars, about home, roads and everyday life. It shares its acoustic storytelling with folk and its roots with the blues.

## edm
related: synthwave, pop
typical_energy: 0.92
Electronic dance music is built for dance floors and festivals, with big synthesizer leads, build ups and bass drops. Synthwave is its retro cousin, and it shares its hooks with pop.

## r&b
related: hip hop, jazz, pop
typical_energy: 0.50
R&B is smooth, groove based music centered on soulful vocals. It shares its rhythm with hip hop, its chords with jazz and its melodies with pop.

## folk
related: country, blues
typical_energy: 0.30
Folk is quiet acoustic music, usually a voice and a fingerpicked guitar telling a personal story. Country is its closest neighbor, and the blues shares its plain, emotional honesty.

## reggae
related: latin, hip hop
typical_energy: 0.55
Reggae is laid back Jamaican music with offbeat guitar chops and deep, easy basslines. It shares its Caribbean rhythms with latin music and its bass weight with hip hop.

## blues
related: jazz, folk, rock
typical_energy: 0.45
The blues is slow, expressive music about hard times, built on slide guitar and a weary voice. Jazz and rock both grew from it, and it shares its honesty with folk.

## latin
related: reggae, pop
typical_energy: 0.85
Latin music is percussion heavy dance music with brass, congas and fast rhythms, including salsa and reggaeton. It shares its Caribbean roots with reggae and its hooks with pop.

## k-pop
related: pop, edm, hip hop
typical_energy: 0.80
K-pop is Korean pop music: highly produced, upbeat and choreography driven, mixing pop choruses with edm drops and hip hop verses. The catalog has no k-pop songs, so pop, edm and hip hop are the closest substitutes.

## punk
related: rock, metal
typical_energy: 0.90
Punk is fast, raw, short guitar music with shouted vocals. The catalog has no punk songs, so rock and metal are the closest substitutes.

## house
related: edm, synthwave
typical_energy: 0.85
House is four on the floor electronic dance music with a steady kick drum. The catalog has no house songs, so edm and synthwave are the closest substitutes.

## soul
related: r&b, blues, jazz
typical_energy: 0.50
Soul is emotional, gospel rooted vocal music. The catalog has no soul songs, so r&b, blues and jazz are the closest substitutes.

## afrobeats
related: latin, reggae, hip hop
typical_energy: 0.75
Afrobeats is West African pop with rolling percussion and warm, danceable grooves. The catalog has no afrobeats songs, so latin, reggae and hip hop are the closest substitutes.
