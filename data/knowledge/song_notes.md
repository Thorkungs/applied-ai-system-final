# Song liner notes

One short note per song in `data/songs.csv`, describing how it sounds and when
people play it. `song_id` links each note to its catalog row. These notes are
hand written for this project, like the song catalog itself.

## Sunrise City
song_id: 1
Bright synth hooks and a sing-along chorus about waking up in a new city. Made for morning commutes, getting ready to go out, and road trips with the windows down.

## Midnight Coding
song_id: 2
A dusty drum loop and warm electric piano, mixed quietly so it stays in the background. Written for late night programming and homework sessions.

## Storm Runner
song_id: 3
Driving guitars and pounding drums that build like a thunderstorm. A favorite for running, sprints and heavy lifting.

## Library Rain
song_id: 4
Soft piano over recorded rainfall and turning pages. Calm background for reading, studying and rainy afternoons.

## Gym Hero
song_id: 5
Punchy bass and shouted hooks engineered for the last rep. Built for the gym, workouts and pre-game hype.

## Spacewalk Thoughts
song_id: 6
Slow, drifting pads with no drums at all. For meditation, winding down before bed and falling asleep.

## Coffee Shop Stories
song_id: 7
Brushed drums, upright bass and a mellow saxophone. Sunday morning coffee, brunch, cooking dinner and quiet conversation.

## Night Drive Loop
song_id: 8
Pulsing retro synths and a neon atmosphere. Made for driving through the city at night and late walks alone with your thoughts.

## Focus Flow
song_id: 9
A steady, unobtrusive beat with no vocals, designed for deep work. Writing, spreadsheets, exam prep and long study sessions.

## Rooftop Lights
song_id: 10
Jangly guitars, handclaps and a feel good chorus about summer nights with friends. Rooftop hangouts, sunny afternoons and picnics.

## Concrete Crown
song_id: 11
Heavy bass and a swaggering flow about earning your place. Walk-in music before an interview, a presentation or a big game.

## Iron Cathedral
song_id: 12
Double kick drums, downtuned riffs and screamed vocals. For releasing anger and for the hardest workouts.

## Moonlit Sonata Redux
song_id: 13
A slow, sorrowful solo piano piece. Quiet reflection, grieving and rainy nights alone.

## Dusty Back Roads
song_id: 14
Steel guitar and storytelling about growing up in a small town. Long highway drives, backyard barbecues and memories of home.

## Laser Bloom
song_id: 15
A huge festival drop and a soaring synth lead. Dance floors, parties and celebrating.

## Velvet Hours
song_id: 16
Smooth vocals over a slow groove. Date night, candlelit dinners and slow dancing.

## Cabin Letters
song_id: 17
Fingerpicked acoustic guitar and hushed vocals about missing someone. Heartbreak and lonely winter evenings.

## Island Time
song_id: 18
Offbeat guitar and an easy bassline. Beach days, vacations, hammocks and sunshine.

## Rainy Day Blues
song_id: 19
Slide guitar and a weary voice singing about bad luck. Rainy days, feeling down and late nights at the bar.

## Fuego Nocturno
song_id: 20
Brass stabs, congas and rapid percussion. Dancing, salsa nights, parties and celebrations.
